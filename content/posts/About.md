This is my personal blog, and is an assortment of my ramblings and thoughts on various subjects. Sometimes I will post my more polished work here too, but that will be marked as such and also posted on other platforms. Everything here that is not marked as polished should be taken with a grain of salt, as it is a work in process, and not meant to be a definitive or final statement of my beliefs. Maybe I'll be a good rationalist and put an epistemic status indicator on top of my posts, but in lieu of that, take this to be the default status of my posts.
